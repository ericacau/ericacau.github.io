## About me

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in the National Ph.D. Program in Artificial Intelligence for Society at the University of Pisa and a Research Fellow at KDD Lab (ISTI-CNR) in Pisa.

My research focuses on LLM-based social simulations, with the aim of understanding the biases and behavior of LLM agents when included in complex social scenarios, such as social networks.

I also study online discussions to understand how polluted information environments, such as echo chambers, emerge and evolve.

## Research interests

### Large Language Models

LLM agents, opinion dynamics, and social simulations.

### Social Network Analysis

Data-driven analysis of interactions and information diffusion in online social networks.

### Natural Language Processing

Computational methods for analyzing language, discussions, and online communities.
