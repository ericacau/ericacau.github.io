## About me

I'm a Ph.D. candidate at the National Ph.D. program in AI for Society at the University of Pisa and a Research Fellow at the KDDLab (ISTI-CNR) in Pisa.

My research focuses on LLM-based social simulations, with the aim of understanding the biases and behaviour of LLM agents when includex in complex social scenarios (e.g., social networks).

Another research interest is detecting and analyzing online discussions to understand how polluted information environments, such as echo chambers, emerge and evolve.
