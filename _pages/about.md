## About me

I am a Ph.D. student in Artificial Intelligence in the National Ph.D. Program in Artificial Intelligence for Society at the University of Pisa and a Research Fellow at ISTI-CNR.

My research focuses on detecting and analyzing online discussions to understand how polluted information environments, such as echo chambers, emerge and evolve. I also study how Large Language Models can be used to simulate the effects of information pollution and opinion dynamics in online social networks.

I am a member of the **KDD Lab**, a joint initiative of ISTI-CNR and the University of Pisa.

## Research interests

### Large Language Models

Large Language Models applied to opinion dynamics and social simulation.

### Social Network Analysis

Data-driven analysis of interactions in social networks using Network Science and Natural Language Processing.
